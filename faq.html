Frequently Asked Questions

How do I order?

Pick an item in the shop or submit a custom request. We confirm details and total by email.

What payment methods do you accept?

Manual payments only: Cash App, Venmo, Zelle and PayPal Friends & Family. There is no online checkout; we send payment details after confirming your order.

When do you start printing?

After payment is received and any design details are confirmed.

How long does it take?

Most items ship in 3–7 business days. Custom work depends on complexity; we give an estimate with your quote.

How does shipping work?

We ship via USPS or UPS with tracking. Local pickup may be available. Shipping is quoted per order.

What materials do you use?

PLA, PETG, ABS, TPU (flexible), Nylon and resin. Tell us your needs and we’ll recommend one.

What file types can I upload?

STL, OBJ, 3MF and STEP. No file? Send a sketch or photo and we can help design it.

What is your refund policy?

Custom items are non-refundable once printing starts. If an item arrives damaged or defective, contact us within 7 days and we’ll reprint or refund.
